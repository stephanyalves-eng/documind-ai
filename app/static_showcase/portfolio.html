Compare proposals with page-level evidence

A local document-intelligence tool for reviewing six commercial fields, tracing claims to the PDF and escalating ambiguous terms.

The procurement problem

Two proposals can show the same price and delivery window while imposing different payment obligations. DocuMind extracts and compares the terms, keeps the original quotations visible and lets a reviewer record a correction with a reason.

What the expanded evaluation exposed

The v2 benchmark covers 17 synthetic PDFs and 18 annotated pairs, including Brazilian formats, tables and multiline conditions. A payment clause with a separate acceptance requirement previously received a false match. The corrected parser now preserves both lines and sends the comparison to review.
